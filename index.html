About the Studio

Small dot, big ideas.

SimpleDot Studio started as a single dot on a blank canvas — an idea that great software doesn't need to be complicated to be powerful. We design, build and publish Android apps, casual games and home-screen widgets that feel simple, fast and full of personality.

Monster Mash: Tap Madness is our very first release, and it's just the beginning. Every future app goes through the same filter: does it spark a little joy? If yes, it ships.

  • Indie-built, community-loved
  • Lightweight apps, zero bloat
  • Fresh colorful UI in everything we ship
